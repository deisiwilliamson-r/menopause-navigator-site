Information Sheet for a Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) Research Project

DePaul University, School of Nursing

Project Title: The Menopause Navigator: A Tool to Help Women in Midlife Make Health Choices That Feel Right for Them
Project Lead: Deisi Williamson, MPH, MSN, RN, FNP-DNP(s)  |  menopausenavigator@gmail.com  |  (312) 373-0216
Faculty Advisor: Dr. Stephanie Byrd, DNP, APRN-FPA, FNP-BC, CNE  |  SBYRD10@depaul.edu

We are asking you to take part in a research study. It is a Doctor of Nursing Practice (DNP) project at DePaul University.

This study is for people who were assigned female at birth, are age 40 to 65, and are going through menopause. It is also for people who have been affected by the justice system and are now living in the community. That means you have been in jail or prison, you are on parole or probation, you have an open or upcoming court case, or you are in a court program like drug court or diversion.

Women, transgender men, and nonbinary people who were assigned female at birth are all welcome.

Please do not take part while you are in jail or prison, or while you are being held waiting for a court date. This study is only for people living in the community.

Please read this before you decide. You can ask questions any time.

Key information: start here

What it is: A short DNP research study testing a free online tool that explains menopause and helps you think through your options.

What you do: Answer a short set of questions on your phone, go through the online tool, then answer a few more questions. Eight weeks later, we send you one more short survey (about 10 minutes) to see how you are doing.

How long: About 45 minutes the first day. About 10 minutes 8 weeks later.

Risks: Low. Some questions may bring up hard feelings. You can stop at any time.

What you get: A total of $25 in gift cards, given in two parts (a $10 card, then a $15 card); a personal summary of your menopause priorities and the options you are considering, which you can keep or share with a provider; and a list of free Chicago clinics.

Your choice: Saying yes is voluntary. Saying no or stopping later will not affect any services you receive. Your answers are private: no names, no records.

The rest of this sheet has more details. You can read on or stop here and decide.

Why we are doing this research

Going through menopause can bring hot flashes, trouble sleeping, brain fog, and mood changes. For women who have lived through prison, jail, or the stress of legal trouble, menopause often starts younger and feels stronger, and there is not enough care made for women like you. We built a free online tool called the Menopause Navigator to help. This study will show us if the tool helps women feel more sure about their health choices and more in control of their care.

What will happen

Everything happens on your phone, tablet, or computer. No in-person visit, ever.

If you do not have your own device, you can use a tablet or computer at a community organization like WIND or at a public library. Contact us and we can help you find a place to take part.

  • Step 1: A first set of questions about your symptoms, your health choices, and how you feel (about 10 to 15 minutes).
  • Step 2: The Menopause Navigator tool, with four short modules (about 25 minutes). You can pause and come back any time.
  • Step 3: A short set of follow-up questions right after the tool (about 5 minutes).
  • Step 4: One last check-in by text message 8 weeks later, a single short survey (about 10 minutes) asking how you are doing.

Technical help

If the survey will not load, a link is broken, or something goes wrong while you are taking part, text or call (312) 373-0216 or email menopausenavigator@gmail.com and we will help you. You do not have to give your name.

Risks

This study is low risk. There is no medicine, no exam, no in-person visit. Some questions may bring up hard feelings about your body, your past, or your healthcare experiences. You can take a break, skip a question, or stop at any time. If you ever need someone to talk to, you can call or text 988, the free, 24-hour crisis line, or call the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233. These are also linked on every page of the tool.

Benefits

You may benefit by:

  • Learning what menopause is and what your options are
  • Getting a personal summary of your menopause priorities and options to keep or share with a provider
  • Getting a list of free and low-cost menopause care in Chicago

Payment

You will get a $10 electronic gift card after you finish the questions right after the tool (Step 3), and a $15 gift card after the 8-week survey. That is a total of $25 in gift cards, not $25 for each survey. When you finish each survey, your gift card appears right on the screen as a one-time link you can use to pick your card. You do not need to give us your name, your phone number, or any other contact information to be paid. If you stop early, you keep what you have already earned.

Your choice

Being in this research study is your choice. You can say no, or stop after starting, without giving a reason. Choosing not to be in the study, or stopping later, will not change any services you get from any organization, your probation or parole officer, or any other person or program. Once you submit your responses, the Project Lead will not be able to remove them later, because all responses are anonymous and we cannot tell which response belongs to you.

Privacy

Only the Project Lead and the Faculty Advisor will see the answers. Here is how we keep your information safe:

  • We do not ask for your name, address, or legal history
  • The survey platform does not save your IP address or location
  • Your answers are stored with strong security on DePaul-protected systems
  • To connect your answers today with your answers in 8 weeks, you will make a short personal code using four small facts about yourself (like the day of the month you were born and the first two letters of the city where you were born). The code is yours alone; we cannot use it to find out who you are
  • If you want the 8-week text, you give your phone number on a separate sign-up step. It is kept in its own list, apart from your answers and your personal code, and is used only to send you the reminder. It is never connected to your answers, and it is deleted at the end of the project
  • Any results we share will only show group patterns, never anything that points back to you
  • Your responses will not be used for any future research and will not be shared with other researchers outside this project

This is for learning, not medical advice

The Menopause Navigator is for learning only. It does not provide medical advice and does not replace care from a healthcare provider. If something does not feel right, please talk to a healthcare provider you trust. The tool gives you a list of free and low-cost places in Chicago where you can get this care.

The information in the Menopause Navigator is based on national menopause guidelines from The Menopause Society, the U.S. Office on Women’s Health, and the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force. The tool’s content was reviewed by the Project Lead and her DNP project committee, who are board-certified clinicians, as part of standard DNP project oversight, so the content reflects current evidence. You can see the full list of sources inside the tool, in the “About this tool” section.
